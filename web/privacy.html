Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Combined Events Calculator ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the iOS app or the website.

What stays on your device

Scores, settings, and other app data are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers for this data, and we do not upload your calculations, saved scores, or settings.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or payment card details.

Tips and in-app purchases

If you leave a tip in the iOS app, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

The app uses RevenueCat to record that a tip was purchased. RevenueCat assigns an anonymous ID and stores purchase history so the purchase can be confirmed and so we can see tip activity in aggregate. That information is not linked to your name or email, and it is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

If you leave a tip on the website, the payment is processed by PayPal. PayPal's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

What this is used for

Purchase history is used for app functionality (confirming a tip went through) and analytics (seeing that tips were made). It is not used for advertising or tracking.

Deleting the app

If you delete the app, data stored on your device is removed. Apple and RevenueCat may still keep records of a completed purchase, as required to process that transaction.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, leave a review on the App Store from the iOS app (More → Leave a Review).
